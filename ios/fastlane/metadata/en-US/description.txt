LocStreamer quietly records where you go during the day and keeps it as a
simple, private location history you can read back any time.

Sign in with your phone number, allow location access, and that is it. The app
records your position continuously in the background and streams it to your own
private storage. There is no map to fiddle with and no social feed: just a
clean, honest list of where you have been, newest first, with the time of each
fix.

Your history is keyed to your phone number and is yours alone. Whenever you
want, tap Delete my data to permanently erase everything from the server.

Features
- Continuous background location recording
- Phone-number sign-in with a one-time SMS code
- A plain, readable list of your recorded points
- One-tap permanent deletion of all your data
