LocStreamer Privacy Policy

Last updated 11 September 2026.

LocStreamer records where your device goes so you can review your own location history. This policy explains what it collects and how it is used.

What is collected

  • Precise location — latitude, longitude, timestamp, and when available the horizontal accuracy and speed reported by your device.
  • Your phone number — used only to sign you in (by SMS one-time code) and as the key under which your own location history is stored and retrieved.

How it is used

Location data is sent to the app's own server and stored so that you, signed in with your phone number, can read back your history. It is used for no other purpose. It is not sold, and it is not shared with third parties for advertising or analytics.

Service providers

Location history is stored in Amazon Web Services (S3). Sign-in text messages are delivered by an SMS provider solely to send your one-time code. These providers process the data only to provide their service.

Retention and deletion

Your location history is kept until you delete it. You can permanently delete all of your data at any time from within the app: open the app and tap Delete my data. This removes your entire location history from the server and signs you out.
